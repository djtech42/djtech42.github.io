03

On the side

What I’ve been building after hours in 2026. Most of these live in private repositories for now.

Retro sound for modern DAWs

AU · VST3 · macOS

A family of audio plugins that model how classic game hardware actually made sound, down to the timing quirks of the original sound drivers, so they can be played from Logic like any other instrument.

Jitter
A Sega Genesis DAC sampler with the irregular, once-a-frame stutter of the Z80 sound driver. Import a game’s VGM rips and it builds drum kits, timing profiles, and MIDI of each song’s parts.
Copper
An Amiga sampler that plays through Paula’s period table and quantizes MIDI to ProTracker’s ticks. Converts MOD files and takes tracker commands over MIDI CC.
Beep
An SN76489 PSG synth that plays the PSG parts Jitter extracts, frame for frame beside its drum kits, with per-channel outputs, scopes, and a spectrogram.
Grit
Just getting started: an effect that runs any audio through the Genesis DAC chain, from 8-bit sample-and-hold to the ladder distortion and the console’s analog filter.

C++20 · JUCE · CMake · Catch2 · Swift analysis tooling
